# DIM Founding Collective

The underlying DIM strategy already describes the platform as an integrated ecosystem combining learning, community, creator commerce, career development and innovation, so Elite should bring those experiences together rather than separating them into different products.

DIM ELITE

Founding 1,000 Beta

The complete DIM experience. Free for the first 1,000.

Learn. Build. Connect. Earn. Grow.

1. What DIM ELITE is

DIM ELITE is the Founding Beta membership of Digital Intelligence Marketplace.

The first 1,000 members receive access to the core DIM ecosystem without paying a subscription during the beta period.

There is no fixed beta end date at launch.

Instead, DIM can evolve the beta based on:

member feedback

platform development

feature readiness

community growth

product validation

business milestones

The first 1,000 members retain their special Founding Member status.

Founding Member status

Every qualifying member receives:

DIM ELITE

FOUNDING 1,000 MEMBER

This status should be visible on:

their profile

community posts

achievements

member directory

selected events

future recognition systems

26. NO FIXED BETA END DATE

The UI should say:

"Elite Beta is currently available to the first 1,000 founding members."
